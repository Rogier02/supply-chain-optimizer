# Project Proposal
This is the project proposal for the supply-chain-optimizer application

## Project Overview
The supply-chain-optimizer will focus on blending a BOM analysis and cost saving application with a sustainability optimizer. Allowing companies to make informed decisions on how to improve the quality, sustainablity, and cost efficiency of their products.

## Problem Statement
Companies today face a massive influx of sustainability and ecological regulations and directives coming out of the European Union and other Governing bodies. These Regulations will be implemented and enforced over the coming decades. Making it important for companies to "Greenify" their products and production lines in order to adhere to the new regulations 

## Objectives and Success Criteria
The supply chain optimizer will help analyse cost efficiency but primarily report on optimizations that can be made to improve product sustainability for companies. Allowing companies to make informed decisions on how they can improve product quality, life-expectancy and lifecycle, sustainability, ecofriendliness, and even ethical compliance.

This will be achieved through the products 'Green-Score' funcitonality. Attributing a sustainability score to the product and clearly show how the product can be improved to achieve better quality and sustainability. 

## MVP Scope
The following is a list that will define the scope of the MVP. Specifically what is in and out of scope and what the priority for the project is

### In Scope
- Import a Multi-level or any relevant BOM in csv format
- Import additional client/supplier/product information, quotes, purchase-price, lead-time, country, logistics, audits, certification data. 
- Take comments and specifications on data the client specifically wants and their goals
- Calculate current cost and carbon baselines and 
- Flag or identify high risk components and supply-chain factors.
- Present high fidelity reporting for clarity, transparency and reliability
- Present easy to digest statistics, alternatives, and other relevant information that might pertain to the specs of the clients request
- Run alternative sourcing scenarios and present cases/analysies on potential optimiziation of costs, sustainability, quality and ethical compliance along with relevant data.
