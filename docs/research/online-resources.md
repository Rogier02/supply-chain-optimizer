# Online Resources

## Environmental regulations that affect supply chains
Resources underlining the changes and enforced regulations to improve the sustainabilty practices of products and businesses:

#### Directive Concepts
Sourcing and raw materials; Digital Product Passport (DPP) & Chcemical Restricitons. Supplies must trace and disclose specific minerals, recycled content percentages and hazardous "substances of concen" via scannable product identifiers.

Product Design and R&D; Mandatory Ecodesign Standards. Engineers must design electronics for extended lifetimes easier physical repair, modular component replacement and ultimate recyclablility rather than planned obsolescence.

Manufacturing & Assembly; Production Disclosures & Bans. Facilities face prohibitions on destroying unsold surplus goods, restrictions on toxic processing chemicals that impair recycling, and demands for verified factoy-floor energy efficiency.

Marketing & Retail; Anti-Greewashing (ECGT). Retailers and brands are barred from using vague terms like "eco-friendly: or unverified carbon-offset climate claims, requiring hard, science-based substantiation for any green label.

End-of-Life & Recovery; Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) Manufacturers must fund and organize take-back infrastructure, ensuring high collection and recycling rates for discarded devices.
